Revenue Analytics & Global Data Platform
This project involved the analysis, integration, and development of a global data and analytics solution for the Revenue organization, designed to consolidate commercial information from multiple ERP systems across different countries and transform it into actionable intelligence for sales, pricing, promotions, distribution, customer management, and revenue strategy. The solution established a cloud-based Data Warehouse/Data Lake environment as a centralized foundation for global commercial information and provided dashboards, graphical analytics, and business enablers that allowed users to move from consolidated performance indicators to detailed analysis of the underlying business drivers.
One of the most significant challenges was data quality and cleansing. Information originated from multiple ERP environments operating across different countries, with differences in structures, definitions, formats, and business processes. This was particularly challenging for commercial information related to prices and promotions, where inconsistencies in source data could directly affect the interpretation of revenue and sales performance. The solution therefore required substantial work to standardize, cleanse, reconcile, and integrate information before it could be reliably exposed through the analytical layer.
The architecture established interconnections between several ERP systems around the world and the cloud-based Data Warehouse/Data Lake, creating a consolidated data foundation for the Revenue organization. This allowed information that previously existed across geographically distributed enterprise systems to be analyzed within a common framework, supporting global visibility while preserving the requirements of individual markets and data sources.
The analytical solution covered multiple dimensions of commercial performance, including sales, pricing, promotions, strategic distribution, key customers, revenues, and competitive information. Dashboards and graphical indicators were designed to provide both high-level management visibility and the ability to investigate the factors contributing to business results. The resulting environment supported more informed analysis of commercial performance and helped the organization develop strategies around pricing, promotion, distribution, customer management, and revenue growth.
Data privacy and regional regulatory requirements introduced an additional architectural challenge. The level of customer and transactional detail that could be exposed differed between regions, particularly between European environments vs for instance Mexican environment, based on different regulations. This required the solution to accommodate different levels of data granularity and different analytical capabilities depending on the applicable regional requirements. In Mexico, the permitted level of detail enabled more extensive drill-down capabilities, while the European context required stricter limitations on the exposure and analysis of detailed information. As a result, the solution required careful design across the complete data lifecycle, from integration and data modeling through to dashboards and presentation.
The project was delivered using an Agile approach and achieved significant results within the global implementation program. Mexico became the only country among the approximately eight initial implementations to complete the full project scope. The complete implementation was delivered in less than one year. Within the first four months, the project delivered full scope for stage one, exceeding the level of functionality delivered by other countries while using a portion of the budget than others.
The demonstrated business value and delivery results led to a extension of the engagement. This extension reflected the continued demand for the capabilities delivered by the solution and allowed the team to further develop the platform and its business value.
Overall, the project transformed geographically distributed and heterogeneous commercial data into a common cloud-based analytical foundation for the Revenue organization. By combining global ERP integration, data cleansing and governance, privacy-aware architecture, analytical dashboards, and business-focused data enablers, the solution provided a scalable environment for understanding revenue performance and supporting decisions around sales, pricing, promotions, distribution, customers, and competitive strategy.

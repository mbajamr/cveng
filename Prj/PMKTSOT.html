Portal MKT-Slots-Orders Totals
This project developed an integrated Marketing information portal that consolidated data from the company's ERP and Oracle databases into a centralized reporting environment for Marketing, senior management, and executive leadership. The system transformed diverse operational and commercial data into standardized indicators, reports, tables, and graphical dashboards, providing a common source of information for monitoring orders, shipments, market demand, and commercial opportunities.
The solution integrated information from Oracle tables and BAAN ERP structures, with Oracle Forms used to control and maintain the underlying information and Oracle-based reporting processes used to generate the required analyses. The resulting reports were integrated into an HTML/JSP portal using Oracle Portal, providing users with a centralized interface for accessing both detailed reports and graphical indicators.
A key component of the project was a new demand-projection management system. Marketing specialists were able to maintain their market-demand projections within the system, while the solution captured actual results and calculated indicators to measure the accuracy of those projections. This created a feedback mechanism between projected demand and actual business results, allowing Marketing to monitor forecasting performance and identify areas requiring improvement.
The system also established an interface between Oracle data structures and tables designed within the BAAN ERP environment, enabling the generation of consolidated commercial indicators. These indicators were used at multiple organizational levels, from Marketing specialists to senior management and the company's General Manager, providing visibility into the company's commercial performance and demand outlook.
The portal included reporting capabilities for key processes such as received orders, shipments, promised orders (PAHO), CM review opportunities and follow-up, quoted proposal details, number of bids, and customer feedback. Graphical indicators and detailed reports allowed users to move from high-level performance information to the underlying operational details.
Security and information governance were incorporated into the portal through role-based permissions. Rather than requiring IT to manage every access change, responsibility for user permissions was delegated to the Marketing department, allowing authorized Marketing personnel to control who could access specific information. This reduced administrative dependency on IT and enabled faster updates as organizational responsibilities changed.
Overall, the project created a controlled business-intelligence layer between ERP data and Marketing decision-making. It consolidated information from multiple systems, introduced measurable demand-forecast accuracy, provided executive-level visibility into commercial performance, and gave the Marketing organization greater autonomy over both its information and access management.

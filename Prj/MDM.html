ERP Modernization & Cloud MDM Transformation
This four-year initiative was a major enterprise transformation program focused on modernizing the company's ERP landscape and progressively transitioning critical Master Data Management (MDM) capabilities from the existing environment to the cloud. The program covered core business data domains including inventory, customers, and vendors and formed part of a broader strategy to modernize the company's enterprise applications while maintaining business continuity throughout the transition.
The transformation was designed as a progressive modernization rather than a single migration event. The initial stage focused on a lift-and-shift/replatforming approach, allowing existing MDM capabilities to be transitioned into the cloud while preserving established functionality and minimizing disruption to the business. A subsequent stage was planned around refactoring, providing an opportunity to redesign and optimize the migrated capabilities to take greater advantage of the cloud environment rather than simply reproducing the legacy architecture.
My primary responsibility within the program was to participate in the development and deployment activities supporting these transformation efforts. This involved working across the processes required to prepare, develop, test, coordinate, and deploy changes within the enterprise environment, while supporting the integration and transition activities associated with the broader ERP modernization program.
I also participated partially in the planning committee responsible for coordinating the transformation efforts. This provided exposure not only to the technical execution of individual developments and deployments, but also to the planning and coordination required to manage a large-scale ERP and cloud transformation across multiple business capabilities.
The program required careful coordination between existing ERP functionality, cloud-based services, development activities, deployment processes, and the business teams responsible for the underlying master data. Particular attention was required for MDM domains such as inventory, customers, and vendors because these data sets are shared across multiple business processes and systems. Changes to their architecture and management therefore had to be introduced in a controlled manner while maintaining consistency and continuity across the enterprise.
Over the four years of my involvement, I contributed to the evolution of the program through its development and deployment activities and participated in the planning of the broader modernization efforts. My involvement concluded before all of the planned MDM migrations were completed, so my contribution covered an important portion of the transformation rather than the entire migration lifecycle.
Overall, the initiative represented a long-term transition from a traditional ERP-centered architecture toward a more cloud-oriented enterprise environment. The staged approach—initially replatforming existing capabilities and subsequently moving toward refactoring—provided a path for the organization to modernize its MDM capabilities while managing the technical and operational risks associated with a large-scale ERP transformation.

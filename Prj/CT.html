Control Tower
Control Tower is a web-based supply-chain monitoring and management system developed to improve service levels and reduce delivery lead times in a manufacturing environment. The solution provides a centralized operational view of critical supply-chain activities by integrating information from multiple Oracle ERP modules, including suppliers, buyers, shipments, forecasts, and materials in transit. Instead of requiring users to navigate multiple ERP screens and reports, the system consolidates this information into a single monitoring environment designed to quickly identify potential delivery risks and supply disruptions.
A central feature of the system is its traffic-light monitoring model, which translates complex supply-chain information into an immediately understandable operational status. Green indicators represent situations where forecasted deliveries are expected to be completed on time, while red indicators identify exceptions such as expired due dates, missing materials, or other conditions that could affect delivery commitments. This allows users to focus their attention on exceptions and potential problems rather than manually reviewing large volumes of transactional information.
The system interfaces directly with the Oracle ERP environment and consolidates information from different functional areas into a common operational view. Information related to suppliers, buyers, shipments, forecasts, and materials in transit can therefore be evaluated together, allowing users to understand the relationship between material availability, supplier performance, shipment status, and expected delivery dates.
Security was a significant part of the architecture. Control Tower was designed to inherit and reflect the existing Oracle ERP security model, using Oracle users, responsibilities, and module permissions to determine the information and functionality available to each user. This avoided creating an independent security model disconnected from the company's ERP and helped maintain consistency between the permissions already established in Oracle and those applied within the web application.
The system also incorporated additional configuration capabilities to adapt the information displayed to different organizational roles and operational requirements. Specific setups could be applied to determine how information was screened and presented for each user, allowing the same platform to provide different views according to responsibilities, business functions, and monitoring needs.
The resulting solution provided a centralized control point for monitoring supply-chain execution. By combining ERP information, forecasts, supplier activity, shipment status, and material availability into a visual exception-management environment, Control Tower enabled users to identify potential problems earlier and focus operational attention on the situations most likely to affect delivery commitments.
Overall, Control Tower transformed fragmented supply-chain information into a real-time operational monitoring environment. Its combination of ERP integration, role-based security, configurable views, and traffic-light indicators provided a common platform for monitoring supply risks and delivery performance, supporting faster identification of exceptions and contributing to the organization's objective of improving customer service and reducing delivery time.

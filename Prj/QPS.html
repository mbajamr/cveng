Quality´s Providers Scorecards
This system is an enterprise-wide supplier management and quality reporting platform designed to consolidate, process, secure, and distribute critical supplier information across the Supply Chain organization. It integrates data from multiple sources, including Oracle databases and BAAN ERP tables, and combines a broad range of technologies such as Oracle Forms, Reports and Discoverer, PL/SQL functions and stored procedures, Java, JSP, ASP, HTML, JavaScript, and Unix-based processes.
The architecture is particularly complex because it must support both internal and external users while maintaining strict security boundaries. Internally, the system provides access through the intranet to users across the Supply organization, from management through the Quality department, using Oracle SSO security. Externally, selected information must be made available to suppliers through the Internet; however, direct access to ERP data is not permitted for security reasons. To address this constraint, the system uses separate servers and controlled ASP-based access, with Oracle Reports generating PDF documents that can be safely distributed without exposing the underlying ERP environment.
A key component of the solution is its data-consolidation architecture. PL/SQL stored procedures and Oracle Reports periodically consolidate information from the different source systems into structures that provide functionality similar to materialized views. These processes are orchestrated through concurrent programs that execute Unix code and generate reports according to predefined schedules managed by Supply Chain personnel. This allows operational information to be systematically collected, processed, and refreshed without requiring users to directly interact with the underlying transactional systems.
The reporting architecture provides different levels of flexibility depending on the business requirement. Oracle Discoverer enables users to customize and explore reports, while Oracle Forms and Oracle Reports support highly specific and complex reporting requirements. Java applications running on Oracle Application Server provide additional reporting capabilities for both internal and external users.
The resulting system transforms data from multiple operational sources into actionable supplier-performance information. It supports supplier qualification and evaluation and enables the Supply organization to identify areas for improvement through indicators such as rejections, nonconformities, corrective actions, supplier charges, administrative costs, and rejection indexes, including top-ten rejection and nonconformity analysis. Information is consolidated periodically and reviewed directly by Supply Chain personnel, providing a structured basis for monitoring supplier performance and identifying opportunities for corrective action and continuous improvement.
Overall, the system represents a significant integration layer between legacy enterprise applications, internal business processes, and external supplier communication. Its value lies not only in its extensive technology stack, but in its ability to transform fragmented ERP and operational data into secure, consolidated, and actionable information for supplier quality and performance management.

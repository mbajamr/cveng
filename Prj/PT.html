Perfect Transportation
Perfect Transportation is an end-to-end transportation and supply-chain management system designed to provide visibility and control across the product value chain, from raw-material planning through manufacturing, shipment, transportation, delivery, installation, and final operation at the customer's site. The system integrates information from Oracle databases, the ERP, GPS systems, and external applications to provide a consolidated view of each product's status and location throughout its lifecycle.
A central component of the system is transportation and shipment management. The system allows transportation plans to be created and monitored from the earliest stages of the process, including planned production dates, actual production dates, shipment scheduling, carrier information, delivery dates, and the product's current location. This enables the organization to begin planning transportation before manufacturing is completed and to continuously track the product as it moves through the supply chain.
The system provides daily tracking of each transformer from the raw-material warehouse through production and shipment, continuing through transportation, delivery, installation, oil filling, and start-up at the customer's site. GPS information is incorporated to provide visibility into the product's location while in transit. This end-to-end tracking improves coordination between manufacturing, logistics, transportation, and the customer and provides the organization with timely information when delivery conditions change.
The solution also integrates transportation information with Oracle and ERP data to generate operational and management indicators used to monitor the performance of the value chain. These include on-time delivery, on-time pickup, carrier performance, transportation costs and cost breakdowns, cost per unit, quality claims, and quality results. Reports can be generated for both domestic and international deliveries while accounting for their different operational requirements.
An important architectural challenge was providing controlled access to information for different types of users while maintaining the company's security requirements. The system was integrated with Oracle Portal to provide controlled permissions for capturing, modifying, reading, and deleting information. It also incorporated an alert mechanism capable of generating different types of operational notifications and exceptions.
The system included a customer-facing component that allowed customer-related information and images to be captured through the Internet. Because the Oracle Application Server environment was restricted from directly accessing external Internet sites for security reasons, a separate Internet-facing component was developed using Microsoft IIS, Active Server Pages (ASP), HTML, and JavaScript. The resulting information could be accessed and reviewed from mobile devices, extending visibility beyond the internal enterprise environment.
The system maintained detailed information for each shipment, including customer, destination, transportation unit, dimensions, weight, carrier, planned and actual dates, and delivery status. This information enabled the organization to coordinate complex domestic and international transportation requirements and monitor the movement of products throughout the entire delivery process.
Overall, Perfect Transportation transformed transportation management from a collection of disconnected production, shipment, and logistics activities into an integrated, traceable process. By combining ERP, Oracle, GPS, transportation, and customer information, the system provided a single operational view of the product's journey and gave the organization the information required to monitor delivery performance, transportation costs, carrier performance, quality, and customer service.
